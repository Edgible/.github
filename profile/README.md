# Edgible

Run your own services, such as your AI models, your photos, and your tools, on machines you own, and reach them from anywhere on a real HTTPS address. Edgible handles the tunnel, the certificates, and the routing. You keep the machine and the data. [edgible.ai](https://www.edgible.ai)

Edgible's open-source upstream is in **[edgible-org](https://github.com/edgible-org)**: the [catalog of cards](https://cards.edgible.org), tested recipes for self-hosted apps, the [card-kit](https://github.com/edgible-org/card-kit) and the agent skill, and [cards-submissions](https://github.com/edgible-org/cards-submissions) to add your own.

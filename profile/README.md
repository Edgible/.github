# Edgible

Run your own services, such as your AI models, your photos, and your tools, on machines you own, and reach them from anywhere on a real HTTPS address. Edgible handles the tunnel, the certificates, and the routing. You keep the machine and the data. [edgible.com](https://edgible.com)

## Start here

- **[The catalog of cards](https://edgible.github.io/cards/)**: self-hosted apps, each tested end to end on a real Edgible serving device every week, and what people want to self-host, need by need, with an honest answer on whether a machine of your own suits it.
- **[Edgible/cards](https://github.com/Edgible/cards)**: the cards themselves. Each one runs an app, such as Gitea, Immich, Vaultwarden, or Umami, or wires several together.
- **[Edgible/card-kit](https://github.com/Edgible/card-kit)**: the card format, the tools that check and test a card, and the [edgible-cards agent skill](https://github.com/Edgible/card-kit/tree/main/skills/edgible-cards), which takes an AI agent from a need to a running, published app.
